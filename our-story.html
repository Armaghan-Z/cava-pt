Our Story

CAVA started with four friends, Colby, Ashu, Vasanth, and Armaghan, who all enjoy playing sports. The idea for CAVA came up during their Honors Human Body Systems Class, where they kept noticing the same problem: rehab options are often small-scale, with limited equipment, and not personalized. Colby resonated deeply with this concern. A dislocated shoulder pulled him away from rugby during his last season's playoffs, yet despite accessing commercial rehabilitation services, he dislocated his shoulder again, unfortunately ending his rugby career. So we decided to democratize rehabilitation. CAVA gives athletes and weekend warriors access to a wide network of interdisciplinary experts, free exercise plans, and personalized rehabilitation plans. After a successful Series A and Series B funding round, CAVA plans to IPO within the next sector.
