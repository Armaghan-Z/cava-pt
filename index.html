Who are we?

We're Ashu Yalamarty, Colby Moran, Armaghan Zarak and Vasanth Rajasakeran, a group of student athletes who started CAVA to help other athletes recover from injuries. We know how hard it is to balance sports, school and everything else, and we know what it's like to get hurt or be sore all the time.

That's why we made CAVA. It's an online or hybrid program that teaches simple ways to recover quickly and get back to the sport you love. Whether you play basketball, soccer, baseball or something else, we build your plan around your schedule and what you want.

Physical Therapy 101

What is physical therapy exactly?

Physical therapy is a type of healthcare that helps people get their movement and body function back. It builds strength and stability and lowers pain after an injury or surgery.

A physical therapist checks how your body is doing and makes a plan just for you. That plan uses stretches, exercises, and teaching you about the muscles or bones that are bothering you. The main goal is to help you recover from your injury, surgery or pain and stay healthy long-term.
