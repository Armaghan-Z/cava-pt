Injury prevention

We screen how athletes move, find weak links, and build a plan before an injury happens.

Book a screen What we check

Why prevention

Recovering from an injury like Jackson's takes months. Finding risky movement patterns early can lower the chance of getting hurt at all.

Many sports injuries follow patterns we can measure: stiff ankles, knees that cave inward on landing, shoulders that tire before the end of a game. A short screen shows where those patterns are, and a plan targets them.

We use the same method we use in rehab: measure, personalize, track and progress.

How it works

  1. Screen: a pre-season movement check.
  2. Plan: exercises picked for your results and sport.
  3. Check in: we re-measure every month.
  4. Adjust: the plan changes as you get stronger.

Pre-season movement screen

A one-visit check of the joints and movements your sport depends on.

Mobility

We measure ankle, hip, shoulder and wrist range of motion with a goniometer, the same tool we use in rehab.

Balance and landing

Single-leg balance and a jump-landing test show how well your ankles and knees absorb force.

Strength

Grip strength on a dynamometer and simple strength tests compare your left and right sides.

Basketball injury prevention

Basketball means jumping, cutting and contact at the rim, so we focus on three areas.

Ankle and knee stability

Ankle sprains are among the most common basketball injuries. Balance work and hip and calf strength help the ankle and knee stay steady when you cut or land.

Jump-landing mechanics

We teach soft landings: knees bent, knees in line with the toes, and weight spread across both feet. Knees that cave inward on landing put more strain on the knee.

Shoulder and wrist care

Rotator cuff and forearm strength protect the shoulder and wrist during shooting, rebounding and falls. Jackson's injury happened when his arm caught in the rim.

Personalized training plan

Your screen results decide your plan. Each plan lists the exercises, sets, reps and how often to do them, with simpler options if something hurts.

Plans fit around your team's practice and game schedule.

Monthly check-ins

Once a month we repeat the key measurements, compare them with your first screen, and update the plan.

If a number drops or something starts to hurt, we catch it early.
