Care & Rehabilitation Plan

Jackson's personalized 12-week recovery program — built from his specific ROM data, surgical history, and basketball return-to-sport goals.

Patient Jackson, 20 Procedure ORIF Duration 12 weeks Phases 3
Goals for the Patient

What Jackson wants to achieve

Jackson communicated four clear outcomes at his initial consultation. Every element of the care plan was designed to meet these specific goals.

01

Return to Full Basketball

Jackson's primary goal is to return to unrestricted competitive basketball, including overhead dunking and shooting. He wants to complete his college season without limitations.

02

Restore Daily Hand Function

Regain the ability to write, type, dress, and drive — all restricted post-surgery. Fine motor control is critical for academic performance during recovery.

03

Eliminate ROM Deficits

Restore full wrist extension (−20° deficit), flexion (−30°), and deviation. These are required for proper catching, dribbling, and shooting mechanics.

04

Rebuild Psychological Confidence

Jackson expressed anxiety about re-injury, especially going up for dunks. Regaining mental confidence for high-impact play is as important as the physical recovery.

Multidisciplinary Team

Jackson's care team

A single provider rarely has all the expertise recovery requires. Three specialists each address a different dimension of rehabilitation.

Primary Oversight

Orthopedic Surgeon

Monitors bone union via follow-up X-rays at weeks 2, 6, and 12 post-surgery. Confirms hardware integrity, approves the Phase 1→2 loading transition, and provides final return-to-sport clearance. All significant loading changes require surgical sign-off.

Fine Motor Recovery

Occupational / Hand Therapist

Rebuilds fine motor skills for daily academic tasks: handwriting, typing, using a phone, and dressing. Provides a custom wrist orthotic for study sessions. Critical for Jackson's return to coursework weeks before full PT completion.

Sport Return

Athletic Trainer / Strength Coach

Designs basketball-specific return-to-sport protocol in Phase 3 — progressive on-court drills that rebuild dribbling, passing, shooting, and overhead catch mechanics. Monitors for compensation patterns that could increase re-injury risk.

Beyond Exercise

Additional strategies & interventions

Exercise alone doesn't complete recovery. These evidence-supported interventions address lifestyle, psychological, and recovery-technology dimensions of Jackson's plan.

Assistive Device

Custom Wrist Orthotic

A hand therapist-fitted orthotic holds the wrist in a neutral position during studying and typing, protecting ORIF hardware from rotational stress. Allows Jackson to return to coursework weeks earlier than without it.

Mental Health

Sports Psychology Coaching

Cognitive-behavioral strategies for kinesiophobia (fear of movement) are introduced in Phase 2. Visualization, graded exposure to high-impact scenarios, and confidence-building progressions ensure mental readiness matches physical readiness at return to play.

Recovery Modality

Contrast Hydrotherapy

Alternating warm (38°C) and cool (15°C) water immersion 3× weekly during Phases 1–2. Cycles vasoconstriction and vasodilation to flush metabolic waste and promote nutrient delivery to healing bone and muscle tissue.

Recovery Modality

Low-Level Laser Therapy

Applied at the fracture sites at select in-office visits. Uses 630–1000nm wavelengths shown in research to reduce inflammation and accelerate cellular repair — particularly beneficial near ORIF hardware where direct massage is contraindicated.
